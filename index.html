Why

We de-risk embedded products built for messy, real-world environments.

We don't optimize isolated components. We take responsibility for how the entire embedded system behaves across hardware, firmware, protocols, connectivity, including integration failures.

We specialize in embedded products that must work reliably outside the lab — on factory floors, in plants, in the hands of non-technical users.
